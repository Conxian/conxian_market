## 4. Best Candidate Analysis: SLA Gap Card Auto-Resolution & Reputation Recovery Engine

`docs/knowledge_base/sla_bounty_system.md` defines the complete remediation lifecycle for SLA breaches:
1. **Gap Card Generation**: When an SLA rule triggers, a gap card is created with urgency-based pricing floors.
2. **Auto-Resolution (`autoResolveGapCard`)**: When a gap card is completed and verified, it is marked as `resolved`, triggering automatic escrow settlement and builder reputation adjustment.
3. **Reputation Recovery (`evaluateReputationRecovery`)**: Resolving gap cards rewards the builder with +5 reputation points, accelerating recovery from penalties and restoring TrustTier eligibility.
