# Conxian Vision Alignment Check: The "AI Office" vs. The Marketplace

## 1. Vision: "Multi-Dimensional Scaling"
- **Status:** ALIGNED.
- **Verification:** By moving inference to the edge and using the Hub only for coordination, we ensure O(1) infrastructure cost relative to O(n) revenue growth.

## 2. Vision: "Sovereign AI for All"
- **Status:** ALIGNED.
- **Verification:** The "Sovereign OS" (DD) approach ensures that user data and keys (BYOK) are never centralized, fulfilling the "On-Chain Blackrock" promise.

## 3. Vision: "Incentivizing External Agents"
- **Status:** ALIGNED.
- **Verification:** The "Builder Network" (DD) and "Marketplace" (Conxian Market) are designed specifically to ingest and monetize external AI skills (like Bittensor/Fetch.ai) rather than building proprietary agents.

## 4. Vision: "Automated AI Office"
- **Status:** NEEDS REFINEMENT.
- **Verification:** We have the orchestration pieces, but we need to define the "Business Rules" standard (e.g., Conxian-flavored YAML) to make the AI Office "good enough to run a full business operating system."

## 5. Conclusion
The combination of Conxian's **Settlement Layer** (Wallet/Escrow) and DD's **Orchestration Layer** (CLI/Swarm) creates a unique market position: **The Infrastructure of AI Labor.**
