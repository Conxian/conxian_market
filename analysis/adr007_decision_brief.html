Decision brief · ADR-007

Two products, two rates —
not one rate argued three ways.

The market's take was documented as 0.50%, 2.0% and 10% — a 20× spread. Resolved: those are the correct prices for two different products, under one split rule.

At 0.50%, covering even the lean $24k/yr ask needs $4.8M/yr of volume; at a plausible $1M/yr it earns $5,000. The market prices a marketplace (escrow, attestation, SLA, dispute) like a settlement rail.

The decision — price the two products

Product A · the land product

Rail settlement

  • Raw pass-through: x402 / MCP-402 call, M2M packet, direct transfer
  • No matching, no escrow, no SLA, no dispute
  • Value delivered = transport & routing
ADR-005 dynamic rate
Product B · the expand product

Matched job

  • Job card + escrow + trust-tier attestation
  • + SLA enforcement + dispute resolution
  • Value delivered = the marketplace service
10% take — builder keeps 90%

One split rule

The single rule
Every take is split 50 / 30 / 20 — operations / founders / ecosystem — applied to the platform's take, whatever it is.
Rail → 50/30/20 on the fee F  ·  Matched job → 50/30/20 on the platform's 10%. One rule, two rates.
